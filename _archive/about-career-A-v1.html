Life at Equinox

Where entrepreneurial law meets human balance

Traditional law firms measure a professional’s worth by billable minutes, late-night emergencies, and adversarial posturing. At Equinox Business Law Group, we rebuilt legal practice from the ground up around alignment, transparency, and sustainable impact.

As an attorney, paralegal, or business operations specialist here, you are never an isolated cog in a billable machine. You are a valued strategic partner to ambitious entrepreneurs, working inside multidisciplinary client pods where every team member has dedicated operational support.

Because our clients engage us through predictable, fixed-fee monthly subscriptions rather than hourly friction, our entire firm is aligned on what actually matters: solving problems, anticipating risk, and enabling leaders to go boldly into the unknown.

The Equinox Advantage

A firm culture designed for growth & longevity

Traditional legal practice often forces a choice between sophisticated corporate counsel and personal well-being. At Equinox, we built a better way: predictable fixed-fee partnerships that eliminate stopwatch billing and align our team around proactive client value.

Working inside collaborative client pods with dedicated paralegals and project managers, you advise ambitious business owners directly—with genuine schedule flexibility, continuous mentorship, and zero billable quotas.

Six Pillars

Why legal professionals choose Equinox

Here is how our proactive, subscription-based model translates into a better daily practice:

01

Zero Billable-Hour Quotas

We operate on a fixed-fee subscription GC model. Your performance is evaluated by strategic client value, problem-solving, and relationship depth — never stopwatch increments.

02

Multidisciplinary Pods

You never practice alone. Each matter is supported by a designated Lead Counsel, a Legal Project Manager, and experienced Paralegals, giving you collaborative backing every day.

03

Direct Business Impact

Advise passionate business owners and executive leadership teams on corporate growth, ownership transitions, employment policies, contracts, and IP protection.

04

Flexibility & Autonomy

We are a modern, hybrid-friendly firm. Work autonomously with flexible schedules across Washington, Oregon, Colorado, and surrounding Mountain West jurisdictions.

05

Continuous Mentorship

Receive leadership coaching, CLE stipends, professional development sponsorship, and transparent milestones for legal and operational advancement.

06

Complete Benefits Package

Competitive base salaries, comprehensive medical/dental/vision coverage, 401(k) retirement plan with employer matching, and generous paid time off.
