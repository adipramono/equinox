Community & Education

Legal insight shared in plain English

At Equinox Business Law Group, we believe business leaders make their best strategic decisions when they understand the legal terrain before entering it. Rather than guarding legal knowledge behind billable walls, our events, webinars, and executive roundtables are designed to demystify corporate law and provide actionable frameworks you can implement immediately.

Whether we are unpacking shifting employment regulations across Washington, Oregon, and Colorado, guiding multi-generation family businesses through succession planning, or sharing how to negotiate enterprise contracts, our attorneys focus on practical solutions—never abstract theory or legal jargon.

Explore our upcoming live sessions below, access our library of on-demand recordings, or invite an Equinox attorney to deliver a tailored keynote or workshop for your leadership team.
