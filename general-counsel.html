Traditional Law Firms vs. Equinox General Counsel

Hourly Billing & Reactive Advice

  • Communication friction: Business leaders hesitate to call until an issue has already escalated into a crisis.
  • Transactional disconnect: Attorneys evaluate single documents in isolation without understanding your company’s long-term roadmap.
  • Unpredictable legal budget: Monthly legal spend fluctuates wildly between zero and tens of thousands of dollars.
Equinox Advantage
Equinox General Counsel
Fixed Monthly & Proactive Strategy
  • Predictable fixed investment: All-inclusive monthly rate tailored to your business needs with zero stopwatch fees.

  • Embedded business context: We act as your Chief Legal Officer, balancing risk with commercial growth and revenue goals.
  • Institutional SLA: Guaranteed 24-hour response turnaround ensures your sales deals and executive decisions never stall.
Holistic Legal Coordination

Managing 85%+ of Legal Matters and Coordinating Outside Specialists

Just as your primary care physician manages your overall health and brings in specialists only when necessary, your Equinox General Counsel oversees your complete corporate legal posture under one predictable monthly investment.

From everyday commercial contracting, real estate, and intellectual property to employee equity and corporate governance, we handle the vast majority of business matters internally.

If your enterprise encounters an ultra-specialized issue—such as international patent litigation or complex maritime regulation—we brief, direct, and oversee that specialist. Because we understand your business and manage the scope, you avoid inflated specialist bills and unnecessary hourly runaway.
