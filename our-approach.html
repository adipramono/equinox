Readiness Diagnostic

You’re Ready for Fractional General Counsel When...

Recognizing the critical operational inflection points when your enterprise has outgrown ad-hoc, reactive legal help.

Complexity

Operational & Strategic Growth

  • Business growth introduces complexity in hiring, contracts, commercial leases, and regulatory compliance.
  • Other fractional executive services (like a Fractional CFO or HR partner) are already serving your leadership well.
  • Legal documents are hard to use in practice—agreements feel cumbersome, academic, or stall sales negotiations.
  • Outside advice feels disconnected from your actual company goals and the fast-paced realities of running a business.
Friction

Clarity, Confidence & Costs

  • You need a partner who respects your time, looping you in only when necessary and prioritizing critical risks vs. what can wait.
  • Teams feel intimidated and lack legal fluency—they need an accessible attorney who makes the law empowering, not overwhelming.
  • Risks are presented without context or practical solutions, creating executive anxiety rather than clear decisions.
  • Surprise invoices turn legal help into a liability, where even minor quick questions come with major, unpredictable bills.
