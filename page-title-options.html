Inner Page Title UI Explorations

Comparing 5 distinct treatments for the hero/page title area across Equinox inner pages (e.g. about.html, services.html, team.html). Each option solves the layout balance differently—from clean typography without images, to solid executive colors, to full-width background textures and balanced landscape image cards.

CONCEPT 01

The Pure Editorial • Clean Typographic Authority

Type: No Image. Inspired by premium corporate and legal institutions (McKinsey, Cooley, Wilson Sonsini). Uses refined typographic hierarchy, subtle light gradient backdrop, and a 3-point key credential ribbon. Eliminates awkward image crops and brings immediate focus to the message.

Zero Image • 100% Crisp
