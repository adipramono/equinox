Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our services and fees

Can we hire Equinox for a single contract or transaction without committing to monthly counsel?
Yes. While many clients eventually transition to our Fractional General Counsel model, we frequently handle discrete, individual engagements—such as negotiating a commercial lease, managing an acquisition, registering a trademark, or reviewing an important vendor contract. Every project is scoped with transparent pricing before work begins.
How does Equinox establish fixed fees without surprise hourly billing?
We take the time upfront to understand the business context, objectives, and deliverables. Because our attorneys specialize strictly in business law and handle hundreds of agreements annually, we can accurately anticipate the required effort and commit to fixed, predictable pricing. You never have to hesitate to call because you fear the clock is ticking.
What is the typical turnaround time for contract review or drafting?
Standard agreements are typically reviewed and turned around within 3 to 5 business days. For time-sensitive business negotiations, closing deadlines, or urgent vendor discussions, expedited turnaround is readily coordinated through your dedicated attorney pod.
In which states is Equinox licensed to provide business legal counsel?
Equinox attorneys are admitted to practice in Washington, Oregon, and Colorado. We frequently assist companies headquartered in these states that operate multi-state commercial operations across the United States.
How does Equinox coordinate with our existing CPA, financial planner, and insurance broker?
Proactive legal strategy requires tight coordination with your financial and risk advisors. We regularly collaborate with client CPAs to ensure tax implications of transactions are addressed, coordinate with insurance brokers to verify coverage provisions match contract indemnities, and align with financial planners on succession strategies.
